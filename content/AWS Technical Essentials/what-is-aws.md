## Cloud computing deployment models

- **On-premises**: companies and organizations host and maintain hardware such as compute, storage, and networking equipment in their own data centers.
- **Cloud**: cloud computing is the on-demand delivery of IT resources over the internet with primarily pay-as-you-go pricing. Companies no longer need to manage and maintain their own hardware and data centers.
- **Hybrid**: a way to connect infrastructure and applications between cloud-based resources and existing resources that are not in the cloud

AWS provides cloud computing services.

## Advantages of cloud computing

1. **Pay-as-you-go** - you pay only for the resources used
2. **Massive economies of scale** - you don't need to buy new hardware when neeeding more compute power or storage.
3. **Stop guessing capacity** - you don't need to guess your infrastructure capacity needs, because you can access as much/little capacity as you need.
4. **Increase speed and agility** - you can reduce the time to make resources available from weeks to minutes, increasing agility for the organization.
5. **Cost savings** - remove the "undifferentiated heavy lifting", no more need to maintain data centers. Focus on customers, rather than on racking, stacking, powering physical infrastructure.
6. **Go global** - with support for deploying in multiple Regions around the world, you can provide lower latency and better experience for customers.
