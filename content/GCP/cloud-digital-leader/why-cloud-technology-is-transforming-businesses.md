title: 01. Why cloud technology is transforming businesses
---

**Digital transformation**: When an organization uses new digital technologies, such of public, private, and hybrid cloud platforms to create or modify business processes, culture, and customer experiences to meet the needs of changing business and market dynamics.

Typically there are three types of cloud computing service models available in public cloud:

1. **[[cloud-computing-models-shared-responsibility#IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service)|IaaS]]** - Infrastructure as a service offers compute and storage services.
2. **[[cloud-computing-models-shared-responsibility#PaaS (Platform as a Service)|PaaS]]** - Platform as a service offers a develop-and-deploy environment to build cloud apps.
3. **[[cloud-computing-models-shared-responsibility#SaaS (Software as a Service)|SaaS]]** - Software as a service delivers apps as services; users get access to software on a subscription basis.


## Primary capabilities that form the basis of the transformation cloud

1. **Data** - the key to unlocking value from AI; data cloud lets organizations identify and proces data with great scale, speed, security, reliability.
2. **Open infrastructure** - brings Google Cloud services to different physical locations while leaving the operation, governance and evolution of the services to Google Cloud.
3. **Collaboration** - helps transform how people connect, create, collaborate; **Google Workspace** is a collaboration cloud that brings together communication and collaboration apps like Gmail, Chat, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Meet into a people first experience powered by Google AI.
4. **Trust** - cloud is more secure than on premises.
5. **Sustainable technology** - a transformation cloud is built on sustainable foundation, using technology and solutions that help organizations build and work more sustainable. Google Cloud data centers are 2 times as energy-efficient as a typical enterprise data center.
