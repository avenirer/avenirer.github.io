## Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

This analysis aims to weigh the cost of cloud adoption against the cost of running their current on-premises systems.

**CapEx** - Capital expenditures, are upfront business expenses put toward fixed assets (buy once, benefit later).

**OpEx** - Operating expenditures, are recurring costs for a more immediate benefit (day-to-day expenses).


## Bandwidth and latency

**Bandwidth** is a measure of how much data a network can transfer in a given amount of time (megabits per second - Mbps -, or gigabits per second - Gbps)

**Latency** the amount of time it takes for data to travel from one point to another - delays in communication over a network (milliseconds)

No matter how much data you can send and receive at once, it can only travel as fast as network latency allows.
